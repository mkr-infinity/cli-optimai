# Code of Conduct

v1.3.0 — applies to mkr-infinity/cli-optimai and its community spaces.

## Our standards

- Be friendly and patient; assume good intent.
- Keep feedback on the code and the behavior, not the person.
- No spam, no advertising in issues or PRs, no unrelated self-promotion.
- Respect Telegram support: no mass-pinging.

## Scope

Applies in this repository (issues, PRs, discussions) and in the project's
Telegram channel/group.

## Reporting

Report violations via [Telegram @mkr_infinity](https://t.me/mkr_infinity) or
the BUY-ME link contacts in the README. The maintainer responds within
48 hours and, where necessary, removes violating content and bans
participants.
